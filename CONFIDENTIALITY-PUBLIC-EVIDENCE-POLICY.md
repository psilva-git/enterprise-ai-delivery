# Confidentiality & Public Evidence Policy

This repository is a public professional portfolio. Its purpose is to demonstrate experience, methods, delivery capability and selected engineering / transformation evidence without exposing confidential customer or employer information.

## Public evidence principles

The following rules apply to all public content in this repository:

1. **Public employer and product references may be named** where the employer, manufacturer or another authorized public source has already published the relevant product, customer, application or company relationship.
2. **Confidential customer engagements remain anonymized** unless the customer relationship and relevant project context are already publicly disclosed by an authorized source.
3. **Internal project names, non-public customer relationships and confidential platform names are not published.**
4. **Internal commercial information is excluded**, including non-public budgets, pricing, contractual terms, margin data, detailed cost structures and customer-specific forecasts.
5. **Internal delivery problems are generalized rather than exposed.** Public descriptions may explain that an initiative required stabilization, recovery or transformation, but do not disclose confidential escalations, internal failures, disputes or sensitive organizational details.
6. **Technical evidence is kept at an appropriate level of abstraction.** Proprietary source code, credentials, private infrastructure details, internal architecture documents, customer data and restricted implementation configurations are not published.
7. **Personal contribution is distinguished from third-party ownership.** References to employers, customers, products or Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) do not imply ownership of third-party products or intellectual property.
8. **Illustrative frameworks and portfolio models are labeled accordingly** and must not be presented as specific customer deployments unless that relationship is public and publication is appropriate.

## Practical publication boundary

Public portfolio content may include:

- role titles and responsibilities,
- technologies and methods used,
- publicly documented products and application environments,
- high-level delivery, transformation and governance approaches,
- anonymized customer / industry context,
- generalized outcomes such as improved predictability, transparency, delivery stability or governance maturity,
- independently created reference architectures, frameworks, demos and training projects.

Public portfolio content should not include:

- confidential customer names or project names,
- internal Jira / Confluence content,
- private requirements, specifications or architecture documents,
- non-public financial figures or customer-specific forecasts,
- credentials, tokens, account identifiers or security-sensitive configuration,
- confidential incidents, escalations or commercial negotiations,
- proprietary source code or employer / customer intellectual property.

## Evidence standard

Where named companies, products or application references are used, they are intended as professional context and should be supported by either:

- public employer / manufacturer material,
- another authorized public source, or
- factual employment / role information that can appropriately be stated in a professional CV or portfolio.

Where that standard cannot be met confidently, the reference should be anonymized.

## Guiding principle

> **Show the capability. Protect the customer. Separate public evidence from confidential delivery detail.**
