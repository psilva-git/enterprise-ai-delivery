# Integrated Leadership Model

I do not treat engineering, commercial development and project execution as separate worlds. In complex Business-to-Business technology, they form one management system:

```text
Market / Customer Need
        ↓
Technical Discovery & Feasibility
        ↓
R&D / Product / Solution Definition
        ↓
Business Case & Commercial Positioning
        ↓
Proposal · Negotiation · Commitment
        ↓
PMO / Program / Project Steering
Scope · Capacity · Budget · Forecast · Risk
        ↓
Delivery · Qualification · Customer Outcome
        ↓
Evidence · Follow-up Business · Portfolio Learning
```

This is where I create the strongest value: **understanding the technology, shaping the business opportunity and governing execution through to measurable customer outcomes**.
