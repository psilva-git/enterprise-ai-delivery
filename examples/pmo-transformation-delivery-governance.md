# PMO, Transformation & Delivery Governance

## Executive evidence

This evidence summarizes how I structure **portfolio governance, transformation delivery and executive transparency** across classical, Agile and Hybrid-Agile environments.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../assets/hybrid-pmo-operating-model.svg" alt="PMO, portfolio governance and fit-for-purpose delivery operating model" width="100%" />
</p>

The operating model connects **business strategy and outcomes** with portfolio priorities, programs, products, projects and delivery teams while keeping governance focused on the decisions that matter.

| Governance layer | Management focus |
|---|---|
| **Business strategy & outcomes** | strategic direction, value, investment logic and measurable outcomes |
| **Portfolio / PMO** | priorities, dependencies, risk, capacity, governance and executive transparency |
| **Programs · Products · Projects** | roadmaps, scope, milestones, cross-team alignment and delivery confidence |
| **Fit-for-purpose delivery** | Agile, Hybrid or Predictive models selected according to context rather than methodology dogma |
| **Teams · Technology · Customer Delivery** | execution, adoption and customer value realization |
| **Management control** | KPIs, risk, decisions, escalation and value tracking across the operating model |

My approach is to create **enough governance for reliable decisions without turning governance into operational micromanagement**.

The practical leadership focus is on:

- portfolio priorities and roadmaps,
- resource and dependency transparency,
- risks, escalations and decision boards,
- executive reporting and management cadence,
- KPIs and OKRs,
- Agile / Hybrid-Agile operating models,
- stakeholder and supplier governance,
- change and adoption,
- value realization and continuous improvement.

> **Govern the outcomes, dependencies, risks and decisions — not every task.**

This operating model demonstrates how I connect **strategy, portfolio governance, delivery flexibility, customer value and business performance** in one management system.
