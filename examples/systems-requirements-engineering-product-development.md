## Requirements, delivery and customer value

For me, requirements management is not an isolated documentation activity. It is the connection between **customer intent, system behavior, architecture, interfaces, risks, verification and delivery decisions**.

A strong development environment therefore needs:

- clear requirement ownership and change paths,
- visible interfaces and dependencies,
- risk and feasibility decisions early enough to matter,
- fit-for-purpose modeling and lifecycle methods,
- verification / validation linked to intended system behavior,
- transparent configuration and change control,
- project governance that supports engineering rather than adding bureaucracy,
- customer and supplier communication across the full lifecycle.

This systems perspective is also why I combine **classical, V-model, Agile and Hybrid approaches** according to product context instead of treating one method as universally correct.
