# GreenWear AI Service Assistant

## Public project summary

GreenWear is a practical AI project developed as part of my AI Management qualification. The objective is a **multilingual AI customer-service assistant** for a sustainable fashion brand.

### Topics covered by the assistant

- sustainability and materials
- size / fit guidance
- returns and complaints
- recycling and care
- delivery and general customer service

### Project environment

| System | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Jira** | Backlog, epics, tasks, status and timeline |
| **Confluence** | Governance, guardrails and project results |
| **Atlassian Rovo** | Search and knowledge access across Jira and Confluence |
| **GitHub** | Versioning of prompt, knowledge-base material and tests |
| **ChatGPT** | Running chatbot prototype |

### Working approach

The project uses Jira and Confluence for structured delivery and governance, while approved chatbot content and test material are versioned in GitHub.

The design also includes a **human handover for uncertain or critical cases**.

### Why it is relevant

GreenWear gave me a practical environment to combine AI management, prompting, knowledge management, test thinking and Atlassian-based delivery in one project.

### Public boundary

This page deliberately excludes the detailed prompt, knowledge base, test cases, internal Jira / Confluence content and project-specific configurations.
