# Enterprise AI Platform Advisory

## Executive evidence — market orientation, platform strategy, AI trends and service lifecycle thinking

This workstream evaluates how major enterprise platforms are developing across **work management, service management, workflow automation, data, analytics and AI** — and how those capabilities translate into sustainable digital services.

My strongest practical platform experience is in the **Atlassian ecosystem**, combined with applied work in **Enterprise AI & agents**. For **ServiceNow, OpenText, Microsoft Power Platform, Snowflake, Databricks and Microsoft Fabric / Power BI**, this work demonstrates a **market, portfolio and strategic perspective rather than deep hands-on implementation experience**.

I created the underlying **AI-Platform-Advisory** research workstream to systematically understand how these platforms are positioned, where their capabilities overlap, which trends are becoming relevant, how AI and agents are changing the market, and what this can mean for customer, portfolio, operating-model and investment decisions.

The focus is management decision support rather than single-vendor advocacy. The core question is:

> **Which platform should play which role for which customer, operating model and business outcome — and can the resulting service be operated, governed, improved and scaled economically?**

## Scope

The advisory perspective covers selected capabilities across:

- **Atlassian** — strongest practical platform focus: Jira, Jira Service Management, Confluence, Agile delivery, governance and Rovo-enabled work context
- **Enterprise AI & agents** — practical and strategic focus on governed automation, agentic workflows, interoperability, sovereignty, human review and AI-enabled delivery
- **ServiceNow** — market view of enterprise service management, workflow, CMDB, automation and AI-enabled operations
- **OpenText** — market view of service management, enterprise information, AIOps, software delivery / quality and regulated operating environments
- **Microsoft Power Platform** — market and capability view of low-code automation, workflow and integration
- **Snowflake** — strategic view of managed data, analytics and AI services
- **Databricks** — strategic view of data / AI engineering, governance and enterprise AI capabilities
- **Microsoft Fabric + Power BI** — strategic view of analytics, semantic intelligence, reporting and decision support

## Service lifecycle lens

A platform recommendation does not stop at technical fit. I also consider the service lifecycle:

> **Discover → Design → Deliver → Operate → Improve → Scale**

The management questions include:

- **Customer Value** — does the service solve a durable problem and create measurable outcomes?
- **Service Quality** — can it be operated and improved reliably?
- **Economics** — are capacity, cost-to-serve, tooling and consumption transparent enough to steer?
- **Governance** — are ownership, controls, KPIs, risks and escalation paths clear?
- **Scalability** — can standards, skills and delivery assets be reused across teams and customers?

## What this demonstrates

- structured market and technology landscape assessment
- awareness of current platform and AI trends
- cross-platform strategic comparison
- business-outcome-oriented technology selection
- platform and operating-model thinking
- service and workflow digitalization perspective
- managed-service and lifecycle thinking
- AI governance and enterprise adoption awareness
- commercial, maturity, economics and scalability considerations
- executive communication of complex platform trade-offs

The purpose is to connect **market direction, platform capability, customer value, delivery feasibility, operations, governance, economics and long-term portfolio development** into one decision story — while keeping a clear distinction between **hands-on practical experience** and **strategic market knowledge**.
